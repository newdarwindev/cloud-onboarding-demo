on 2026-10-29. Recheck current official sources before real work; links are research
starting points, not approval to register, accept terms, or configure anything.

These are questions, not claims about requirements for a particular person or
account. The starter does not inspect accounts, register providers, create keys,
submit applications, manage billing, or verify eligibility. Trial/credit programs,
amounts, dates, and eligibility are deliberately not specified or promised.

For any future proposal, research current official terms, record uncertainty, and
seek action-specific owner approval before a verified human performs a manual step.
Do not publish private research, account state, or billing evidence here.
